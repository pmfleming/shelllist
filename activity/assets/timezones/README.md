# World timezone map

`world-time-zones.svg` is a dark-palette adaptation of Wikimedia Commons'
[World Time Zones Map](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:World_Time_Zones_Map.svg)
by Heitordp. The map is in the public domain. Its outlines are based on the
CIA's *Standard Time Zones of the World* map and its timezone information on
the IANA Time Zone Database.

The asset is cropped and recolored for Shelllist. Country/city labels,
political borders, and reference grid lines were removed to keep the compact
view legible. Adjacent UTC bands use alternating high-contrast blue, amber,
teal, and purple families, with bright geographic timezone boundaries that
remain visible when the SVG is scaled down. Geographic regions and boundaries
are unchanged from the source revision downloaded on 2026-09-03.
